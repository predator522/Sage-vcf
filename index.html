FAQ

Is my data secure?

Yes, all data is stored in Firebase Firestore with secure rules. Owner tokens are never exposed.

Do I need an account?

No. You create a session and receive a unique owner token.

What if I lose my owner token?

Keep it safe – it's the only way to manage your session.
